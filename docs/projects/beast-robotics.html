2017 – 2019 DWG

BEAST Robotics: Suction End Effector

Mechanical lead for the end-effector team on FIRST team 2202. I designed a suction-cup effector that captured both game pieces with one system, and won an engineering ingenuity award.

  • FIRST Robotics
  • Autodesk Inventor
  • Molding
  • PETG
  • Award

I joined my high school's robotics team, BEAST Robotics (FIRST team 2202), in junior and senior year.

Senior year I designed the robot's end effector: a suction cup with a replaceable silicone rubber ring, molded from a 3D-printed form. The ring is glued to a printed PETG backing that screws into the cup and seals with an O-ring. The effector is mounted with some compliance so it seats easily against a flat surface.

Our team was the only one of 300+ worldwide to capture both game objects with a single system. We made it to the World Championship, and I received an engineering ingenuity award.

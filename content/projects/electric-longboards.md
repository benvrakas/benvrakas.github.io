The goal was a 3D-printable kit that turns a normal longboard into a powerful electric vehicle for getting around a college campus. High-end commercial boards cost $1,500–3,000; I aimed for about $500.

:::video night-ride | Night ride with LED underglow (2021)

## First build: prototype for a friend (2018)

Printed motor mounts on ¼″ steel rod, plus a printed enclosure for the ESC and wiring. I published the mount files on Thingiverse.

## My own board (2019)

After the prototype worked, I refined the design into a board for myself:

| | |
|---|---|
| Drive | Dual motors |
| Battery | 10S Li-ion, 9 Ah @ 37 V |
| Power | 4,400 W |
| Top speed | 34 mph |
| Charging | USB-C Power Delivery |
| Enclosures | PETG, printed: battery, ESC and charging circuit |

That's more power than the most powerful Boosted Board, at less than half the price.

## Commissioned build (2021)

A friend hired me to build him a board. The focus was a design with a very low failure rate so it would last. Highlights are the printed enclosure and a large **12S4P, 560 Wh** battery.
